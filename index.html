Schedule presets

Calibrate (do this once after mounting)
  1. Press and hold "Turn toward closed" until the slats are fully closed. Let go.
  2. Tap This is CLOSED.
  3. Hold "Turn toward open" until the slats are where you want "open". Let go.
  4. Tap This is OPEN. The board saves how long that took.

Nudge = one small step per tap. Use it for the last bit; it can't get cut off by a Bluetooth drop. You can hold, nudge and stop as many times as you like before tapping a "This is…" button. The distance adds up.

Motor tuning

Cruise = steady speed (lower = slower, but weaker). Kick = short full-power burst to break the rod loose.

⚠ Open/close distances are measured in motor run-time. After you change and save tuning, redo Calibrate so they match the new speed. Tune first, calibrate last.
