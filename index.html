Calibrate (do this once after mounting)
  1. Press and hold "Turn toward closed" until the slats are fully closed. Let go.
  2. Tap This is CLOSED.
  3. Hold "Turn toward open" until the slats are where you want "open". Let go.
  4. Tap This is OPEN. The board saves how long that took.
